How Far in Advance Should You Book a Family Vacation?

A practical timeline for booking resorts, cruises, flights and activities, especially around school holidays and summer.

There is no single right answer, but there are useful guidelines. The best time to book depends on where you are going, when, and how much flexibility you have. Here is how to think about it.

Booking early helps with

  • School holidays and summer. Popular dates sell out first. Families with fixed school schedules usually benefit from booking earlier.
  • Cruises. Cabins, especially family cabins and connecting rooms, can sell out for popular sailings, particularly Alaska in summer and Mediterranean sailings in peak months.
  • Large groups and multigenerational trips. Finding rooms for many people takes time.
  • Special requests. If you want a particular room type, adjoining rooms or special dining arrangements, earlier is better.
  • Land packages and tours. Lodges and guided experiences have limited space.

A rough timeline

These are general ideas, not rules, and they vary by destination. - Cruises and large group trips: several months to a year ahead. - Peak-season resort stays, such as winter in the Caribbean or summer in Hawaii: several months ahead. - Orlando and theme parks: a few months ahead, and sooner for school holidays. - Flexible, off-peak trips: shorter lead times can work, with more chance of good last-minute options.

What about last-minute deals?

Sometimes good deals appear close to departure, particularly for resorts and cruises that have unsold space. But you can end up with fewer choices of rooms, flights and dates. If you have a specific need or date, booking early is usually safer.

Prices can go either way

Prices can drop or rise. Some suppliers allow repricing if a promotion comes out after you book, but policies vary. We watch for changes and ask about your options.

Payments and deposits

Booking early does not mean paying everything at once. Many suppliers take a deposit, with the final balance due closer to travel. Check payment deadlines and cancellation terms.

Book flights wisely

Flight schedules and prices can vary. We coordinate flights with your resort or cruise so that your arrival and departure times work, and so you are not stuck if there is a change.

Passports and documents

The one step you can do before anything is booked is to check passports. Read our post on children's passports.

Start the conversation
