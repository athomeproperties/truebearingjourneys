When Is the Best Time to Take an Alaska Cruise?

Alaska cruise season runs from late spring to early fall. Here is what May, June, July, August and September each offer.

Photo: Peter Hansen / Unsplash

Alaska cruises run for a limited season, generally from around late April or May through September. Within that window, each month has a slightly different personality. Here is what to expect so you can match the month to your family.

May: quieter and cooler

Early season sailings tend to have fewer crowds and sometimes lower fares. Days are already long, the mountains still carry plenty of snow, and wildlife is active. It is also usually the coolest and driest stretch, so pack layers. Some shore excursions and seasonal businesses may be just opening for the year.

June: long days

June brings the longest daylight of the year in Alaska, which means more hours to enjoy the scenery. Weather is often pleasant, and many excursions are running. It is popular with families whose school year has ended.

July: warmest and busiest

July usually offers the warmest temperatures and the most crowded ships and ports, as it lines up with school holidays. It is an excellent month for families who want the fullest range of activities, and it is also when you should book early.

August: salmon and bears

August is a favorite for wildlife. Salmon runs bring bears to the rivers in many areas, and the days remain long enough for outdoor excursions. Rain can be more frequent than in early summer, so a waterproof jacket is worth packing.

September: the quiet end of the season

Early September has fewer crowds and cooler temperatures. School-age families may have trouble traveling then, but it suits grandparents, couples and multigenerational groups who are not tied to the school calendar. Weather is changeable and there may be more rain.

What about the glaciers and whales?

Glaciers are there all season, and whale sightings are possible throughout, with many cruisers reporting humpback whales. Nothing is guaranteed, because wildlife is wild.

Weather reality check

Alaska weather is unpredictable in any month. Dress in layers, bring rain gear, and treat sunshine as a bonus. A cruise that visits several ports gives you more chances for good weather.

Choosing your month

For long days and a school break, many families like June or July. For wildlife, August is a strong choice. For fewer crowds, consider May or September. Read more about the route in our Alaska cruise guide, and see our post on Alaska cruise versus land and sea cruisetour.
