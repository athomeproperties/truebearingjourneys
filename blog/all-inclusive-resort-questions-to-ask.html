10 Questions to Ask Before Booking an All-Inclusive Resort

All-inclusive does not mean the same thing everywhere. Ask these questions to avoid surprises and find the right fit.

The phrase all-inclusive covers a wide range of resorts and experiences. Two properties in the same area can offer very different things for the same price. These questions help you compare.

1. What exactly is included?

Meals and drinks are typically included, but details vary. Ask about premium alcohol, specialty restaurants, room service, snacks and the minibar.

2. Are there extra charges?

Common extras include spa treatments, motorized water sports, excursions, Wi-Fi, and special dinners. Ask what is included and what costs more.

3. What are the kids' programs?

Look at age ranges, hours, activities, and whether there is a charge. Ask whether there is a teen club, and what supervision is provided.

4. What are the room options?

Check how many people each room type sleeps, whether connecting rooms are available, and whether cribs are provided. Some resorts have family suites that make a big difference.

5. How is the beach?

Ask about swimming conditions, whether the beach is protected, and how the resort handles seaweed in the seasons when it appears. See our post on sargassum and your beach vacation.

6. What are the dining options?

Check how many restaurants there are and whether reservations are required. Ask how the resort handles allergies and picky eaters.

7. Is it family-friendly or adults-only?

Some resorts are adults-only, and some areas of family resorts are quiet zones. Make sure the atmosphere matches what you want.

8. How far is the airport?

Transfer time matters after a long flight, particularly with tired children. Ask for the estimated time and whether transfers are included.

9. What is the cancellation policy?

Understand the deadlines and fees, and whether the resort has special terms for weather events.

10. What do recent guests say?

Look at recent, detailed reviews from families with children of similar ages. Resorts change over time, so recent information matters more than older reviews.
