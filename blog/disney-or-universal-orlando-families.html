Disney World or Universal Orlando? How Families Choose

Both Orlando resorts are excellent. Here is a fair comparison for families, by kids' ages, interests and trip length.

Photo: Mick Haupt / Unsplash

Orlando has two big theme-park resorts that families compare most often: Walt Disney World and Universal Orlando Resort. They offer different experiences, and the best choice depends on your children and how long you plan to stay. Many families do both.

Walt Disney World

Disney World has four theme parks, two water parks and a large number of resorts. It is built around characters and stories, and it has the most options for young children. Magic Kingdom is the classic choice for little ones. EPCOT mixes rides with a world-showcase and festivals. Hollywood Studios and Animal Kingdom lean toward bigger thrills and a different style of experience.

  • Strong for: toddlers through early grade-school ages, families who love characters and storytelling, and those who like the idea of a resort stay that is part of the trip.
  • Think about: the scale. Disney World is huge, and it is easy to underestimate how much walking and waiting it involves.

Universal Orlando Resort

Universal has Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure, a water park and, since 2025, Epic Universe. It is known for immersive lands based on movies and books and for intense rides and shows.

  • Strong for: tweens, teens and families who enjoy thrill rides and movie worlds.
  • Think about: height requirements. Some of the best-known attractions have minimum heights, and younger children may not be able to ride them.

Quick ways to decide

  • Kids under about seven or eight, or those who love characters: lean Disney.
  • Tweens and teens who want bigger rides: lean Universal.
  • A mixed group: spend a few days at each, or choose one resort as your base and add a single day at the other.

How many days do you need?

Most families need more days than they expect. A single resort can fill several days, and kids do better with a pool day mixed in. Rather than trying to fit everything in, choose the experiences that matter most to you.

Tickets, passes and skip-the-line options

Ticket products and line-skipping systems change often at both resorts. We check current options at the time we plan your trip so you are not guessing from outdated advice.

Other Orlando options

Orlando also has SeaWorld, water parks and plenty of non-park activities, so there is room for a break between big days.
