Sargassum and Your Beach Vacation: What Travelers Should Know

What sargassum seaweed is, when it tends to show up in the Caribbean, and how to plan a beach trip around it.

Photo: Héctor Mavare / Unsplash

If you have read anything about Caribbean beach vacations lately, you have probably come across the word sargassum. It is a type of floating seaweed that can wash ashore in large amounts on some beaches. It is natural, but it can change how a beach looks and smells. Here is how to think about it when planning.

What it is

Sargassum is a brown algae that drifts across the Atlantic and Caribbean. In some years large amounts reach the coastline of Mexico's Caribbean coast and other islands. When it piles up on the sand and begins to decay, it can produce an unpleasant smell.

When it tends to appear

Amounts vary widely from year to year and from week to week. In general, the heaviest seasons tend to be in the warmer months, roughly spring and summer, while winter is often clearer. But it is not predictable enough to promise any particular week will be seaweed-free, and conditions vary by stretch of coastline.

Resorts handle it differently

Many resorts clean their beaches daily, and some use barriers or other measures to keep the water clear. Beach orientation and local currents matter too, so two resorts a few miles apart can look quite different.

How we plan around it

  • We choose resorts with strong track records for beach maintenance.
  • We look at a resort's pool, spa and onsite options, so the trip still works if the sea is not ideal on a given day.
  • We suggest timing based on your flexibility.
  • We recommend considering the other things the region offers, such as cenotes and ruins.

What you can do

  • Check recent photos from other guests, and ask the resort directly about current conditions before you travel.
  • Choose a resort with a good pool.
  • Pack a flexible attitude. A cloudy-water day can still be a fun pool and excursion day.

Is it a reason to avoid the Caribbean?

Not necessarily. Many travelers have wonderful trips in every season. It is one more factor to weigh, along with hurricane season, price and weather.

Alternatives

If seaweed worries you, we can look at other destinations or at different parts of a region, since conditions are not the same everywhere. Our guides to Aruba, Hawaii and Los Cabos cover places with different conditions. See also Cancun versus Punta Cana.
