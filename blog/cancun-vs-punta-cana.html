Cancun or Punta Cana? Choosing an All-Inclusive Beach Trip

Two of the most popular all-inclusive destinations compared: beaches, activities, travel time and what suits families.

Photo: Héctor Mavare / Unsplash

Cancun and the Riviera Maya in Mexico, and Punta Cana in the Dominican Republic, are two of the most popular all-inclusive destinations for North American families. Both offer warm water, big resorts and plenty of kids' programs. Here is how they differ.

The beaches

Both have beautiful white-sand beaches with turquoise water. Punta Cana's long, palm-lined beaches are often described as more uniform and relaxed. The Cancun and Riviera Maya coast has a wider mix, from calm bays to more exposed stretches. Conditions on any beach vary by resort and season, so we check the specific property rather than the region.

Things to do beyond the resort

This is where the two differ most. - Cancun and Riviera Maya: Mayan ruins such as Chichen Itza, Tulum and Coba, cenotes (freshwater sinkholes you can swim in), snorkeling reefs, Isla Mujeres, Cozumel and eco-parks. It is a rich choice for families who want culture and adventure. - Punta Cana: island excursions like Saona Island, catamaran trips, zip lines, and golf. There is plenty to do, but the focus is more on beach and resort time.

Getting there

Both are served by direct flights from many U.S. and Canadian cities. Flight length depends on your home airport, so compare options from where you live. Transfer times from the airport to your resort can differ a lot depending on which part of each region you choose, which we can plan for.

Resorts

Both destinations have a large range of all-inclusive resorts, from lively family resorts with water parks to quieter adults-only properties. Many family resorts in each place offer kids' clubs, teen programs, and rooms that sleep three or four.

Weather and hurricane season

Both are in the Atlantic hurricane region. Peak storm risk is generally late summer into early fall. Winter and spring are the most popular travel seasons. Travel insurance and flexible booking terms are worth considering for trips during hurricane season.

Seaweed

Some Caribbean beaches, including parts of both coasts, can see sargassum (seaweed) at certain times of year, usually more in spring and summer. It varies year to year and beach to beach. We cover this in our post on sargassum and planning around it.

Which should you choose?

Choose Cancun and the Riviera Maya if you want a beach vacation with ruins, cenotes and variety. Choose Punta Cana if you mostly want a relaxed beach and resort trip. If you cannot decide, tell us your dates and what your kids enjoy, and we will compare specific resorts.
