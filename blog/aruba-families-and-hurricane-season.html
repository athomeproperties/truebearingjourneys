Why Families Choose Aruba (and When to Go)

Aruba's dry climate, calm beaches and location outside the main hurricane belt make it a favorite. Here is what to know.

Photo: Benjamin R. / Unsplash

Aruba is a small Dutch Caribbean island with a reputation for steady sunshine, soft beaches and an easy, welcoming feel. It is a favorite for families who want a beach vacation with fewer surprises.

A drier climate

Unlike many Caribbean islands, Aruba is arid. It gets less rain than most, and the landscape has cacti and rocky desert-like areas as well as beaches. That means a good chance of sunshine on most days of the year.

Outside the main hurricane belt

Aruba lies far to the south in the Caribbean, below the area where hurricanes most often track. Storms can still affect the region, and no destination is risk-free, but it is generally considered a lower-risk choice than islands farther north. This makes it appealing for travelers who want to book in late summer and fall, when other destinations carry more weather risk.

The beaches

Aruba's western and southwestern coast has calm, clear water that is good for swimming and snorkeling. - Eagle Beach is wide and quiet, and the place to spot the island's iconic fofoti trees. - Palm Beach is lively, with resorts, restaurants and water sports. - Baby Beach at the southern tip has shallow, calm water that suits small children.

The wind

Aruba is breezy, which keeps the heat comfortable but can make some beaches feel brisk and the sea choppier on the eastern side. Sunscreen is still important, since the breeze can hide how strong the sun is.

Things to do

Families can explore Arikok National Park, ride ATVs, snorkel shipwrecks, visit a donkey sanctuary, take catamaran cruises and enjoy the island's many restaurants. Kids' menus and casual beach eateries are easy to find.

Where to stay

Palm Beach has the most resorts and nightlife, and Eagle Beach is quieter with a mix of smaller hotels and resorts. Some properties are all-inclusive, while many are not, so check what is included.

Arrival paperwork

Aruba has used a digital arrival form for visitors. Entry requirements can change, so we confirm what is needed for your travel dates and complete the details with you.
