Los Cabos or Cancun? Two Mexican Beach Getaways Compared

Mexico's Pacific tip versus its Caribbean coast: scenery, swimming, activities and when to go.

Photo: Vania Medina / Unsplash

Los Cabos on the Baja Peninsula and Cancun on the Caribbean coast are both popular Mexican vacation spots with plenty of resorts. They look and feel very different, which makes the choice worth thinking through.

The setting

Los Cabos sits where the desert meets the sea. Dramatic cliffs, rock formations and the famous arch at Land's End define the landscape. Cancun and the Riviera Maya are flat and tropical, with jungle, soft white sand and calm turquoise water.

Swimming

Cancun's Caribbean beaches are generally known for gentler, swimmable water. In Los Cabos, many Pacific-facing beaches have strong currents and are not safe for swimming, though there are protected swimming areas, such as certain beaches on the Sea of Cortez side and in resort bays. If your family wants to spend most of the day in the sea, check carefully which beach your resort fronts and ask about swimming conditions.

Activities

  • Los Cabos: whale watching in winter, fishing, boat trips to the Arch, snorkeling at protected spots, desert tours, and golf.
  • Cancun and Riviera Maya: Mayan ruins, cenotes, reef snorkeling, island day trips and eco-parks.

The pool factor

Because ocean swimming can be limited in Los Cabos, many resorts there have excellent pools, and that is often the center of family time.

When to go

Los Cabos is a desert climate. Winter and spring are mild and are the time for whale watching. Summer can be very hot, and late summer into fall carries hurricane risk on the Pacific side. Cancun has a similar warm season with hurricane risk in late summer and fall, and winter and spring are the most popular times.

Getting there

Both have direct flights from many U.S. and Canadian cities. Your home airport and flight times may tip the decision.

Which one fits your family?

Choose Los Cabos for dramatic scenery, whale watching, fishing and a more upscale feel. Choose Cancun and the Riviera Maya for easier swimming and more cultural and nature excursions. If swimming in the ocean is a must for young children, Cancun's side is usually the easier match.
