Cruise or All-Inclusive Resort: Which Is Better for Your Family?

Both are easy, relatively predictable ways to travel with kids. Here is how they differ and who each suits best.

Cruises and all-inclusive resorts are two of the most popular ways to take a stress-free family vacation. They share a lot, including meals, entertainment and activities for children. They also feel quite different. Here is a fair comparison.

How they are similar

  • Most meals are included, which makes budgeting easier.
  • Kids' clubs and family activities are common.
  • Both let you unpack once and have much of the trip organized.

Where they differ

Destinations. A resort keeps you in one place. A cruise lets you see several ports in one trip, but gives you only a few hours in each.

Pace. At a resort you can do very little, or build a day around excursions. On a cruise, port days have a schedule, because the ship leaves at a fixed time.

Choice of activities. A large cruise ship can feel like a floating resort, with water slides, shows and many dining venues. A resort typically has a beach, pools, a few restaurants, and nearby excursions.

Cost. It depends. Both have a wide range, from budget to luxury, and extras can add up on either, such as drinks, excursions, gratuities and spa services. We compare the total cost for your specific options.

Who tends to prefer a resort

  • Families with babies and toddlers who nap on a schedule.
  • Travelers who want to relax in one place and not pack up often.
  • Groups who love the beach and want to enjoy the water most of the day.

Who tends to prefer a cruise

  • Families who want variety and curiosity, and to see several places.
  • Multigenerational groups with different interests, since there is something for everyone.
  • Travelers who like the idea of one trip with several destinations.

Think about the downsides

Resorts can feel repetitive after several days for restless kids. Cruises involve crowds and port days with limited time, and some people dislike the sense of being on a schedule.

How to decide

Ask yourselves what an ideal day looks like. If it is a lounge chair and a pool, a resort is likely a better match. If it is exploring somewhere new each morning, try a cruise. If you cannot decide, tell us your kids' ages, dates and budget.
