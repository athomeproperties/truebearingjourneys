Planning a Family Trip to Jamaica: Choosing Your Area and Resort

Montego Bay, Negril, Ocho Rios and more: how the main resort areas differ, and what to confirm before you book.

Photo: Intyaud Banton / Unsplash

Jamaica offers white-sand beaches, waterfalls, music and some of the Caribbean's best-known all-inclusive resorts. The island's resort areas differ in atmosphere and distance from the airport, so choosing the right one matters.

The main resort areas

  • Montego Bay: home to the main international airport, so transfers are short. It is a good choice for families who want to get to the beach quickly after a long travel day.
  • Negril: known for a long beach and a relaxed vibe, as well as cliff-side views at sunset. It is farther from the airport, so allow for a longer transfer.
  • Ocho Rios: on the north coast, with waterfalls and tropical scenery, including the well-known Dunn's River Falls, and many resorts nearby.
  • Runaway Bay and other north coast areas: quieter stretches with resorts and a more laid-back feel.

Resort style

Jamaica has many all-inclusive properties, including family-oriented resorts with kids' clubs and water parks and others that are adults-only. Check which type you are booking, as the experience differs a lot.

Think about recovery and current conditions

Jamaica was hit by a powerful hurricane in October 2025, and many resorts closed for repairs and reopened in the months afterward. Some properties have had phased reopenings and renovations. Before booking, we confirm that your specific resort is open, that the amenities you care about are running, and what the current situation is in the area.

Safety and travel advisories

As with any destination, check the U.S. State Department's travel advisory before you go. Resort stays are typically straightforward, but it is wise to use trusted transportation arranged through your resort or advisor and to be careful about excursions. Ask us for the latest guidance when you book.

Timing

The dry season, roughly winter into spring, is the most popular time, and prices are often higher then. Summer is hotter, and hurricane season runs from June through November, with the highest risk generally later in that window.

Getting out of the resort

Excursions such as waterfalls, river tubing, catamaran cruises and cultural tours can be highlights. We recommend reputable operators and match activities to your children's ages.
