Popular things to do

A taste of Alaska Cruises

Glacier viewing

Ships cruise to glacier bays and fjords, where ice calves into the water. Bring binoculars.

Whale watching

Humpbacks and orcas are common. Small-boat whale tours from Juneau and Icy Strait Point are popular.

White Pass railway

A scenic narrow-gauge railway journey from Skagway into the mountains, rich with gold rush history.
