Popular things to do

A taste of Los Cabos

Whale watching

From roughly December through April, humpback and other whales pass through. Boat tours are a family highlight in season.

The Arch & Land's End

See the famous rock formations by boat or water taxi, with chances to spot sea lions and seabirds.

Snorkeling at calm coves

Chileno Bay and Santa Maria Bay along the Corridor are popular for snorkeling because of their calmer water.
