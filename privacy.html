Privacy Policy

Plain-language notes on what we collect and why.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

TrueBearing Journeys is an independent travel advisory based in Johnson City, Texas, operated by Craig Verbeck. We work with families to plan vacations. You can reach us at craig@truebearingjourneys.com or +1 (432) 287-8758.

What we collect

When you use our contact form we receive the details you type in: your name, email address, phone number (optional), travel dates, who is traveling, trip type and your message. If you later book through us, we collect the additional information suppliers need, such as names as they appear on travel documents. We do not ask for payment card details on this website.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, plan and book your trip, and follow up about it. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

To arrange your trip we share the necessary details with the suppliers you choose (such as resorts, cruise lines and tour operators) and with Avoya Travel, the host agency we are affiliated with, which supports booking and commissions. Our contact form is processed by FormSubmit.co, which forwards submissions to our email, and our email is hosted by Zoho. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

Our site may use cookies and similar technologies from Google (such as Google Analytics and Google Ads) to understand how the site is used and to measure the results of our advertising. Our language selector also saves your language choice in your browser. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and booking records as long as needed to serve you and meet legal, tax and supplier requirements, then delete or anonymize them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing craig@truebearingjourneys.com. You can unsubscribe from any marketing email at any time.

Children

This site is for adults planning travel. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
