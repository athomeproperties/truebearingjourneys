Our Approach

Your trip, planned the way you'd plan it yourself.

Planning a family vacation means juggling ages, interests, budgets and a hundred small details. We take that weight off you. We listen first, then recommend only the resorts, cruises and experiences that truly fit.

From the first conversation to the day you return home, you have one trusted point of contact for your whole trip.

What you can expect

  • Personalized recommendations, not a list of search results
  • One advisor from first idea to final return flight
  • Access to trusted travel suppliers through our host agency
  • Clear communication on pricing, payments and travel documents
  • Support if plans change before or during your trip

About Us

A guide who knows the way.

TrueBearing Journeys is an independent travel advisory, affiliated with Avoya Travel. In navigation, a true bearing is the exact direction to your destination, and that is what we aim to give every client: clear guidance and a trip that stays on course.

Our founder has traveled extensively abroad over the last 20 years, mainly around Mexico and the Caribbean. That firsthand experience shapes how we choose destinations, resorts and experiences for our clients.
