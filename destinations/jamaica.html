Overview

Why families choose Jamaica.

Jamaica is an island with a big personality: music in the air, vibrant food, mountains rising behind the coast, and some of the Caribbean's warmest hospitality. English is the official language, which makes it comfortable for first-time Caribbean travelers.

Most family vacations center on resort areas: Montego Bay and Falmouth on the north coast, Negril with its long beach and clifftop sunsets, and Ocho Rios with waterfalls and lush scenery. Resorts range from large family-oriented all-inclusives to intimate boutique properties.

Best for

  • Families who want culture and music along with the beach
  • All-inclusive resorts
  • Multigenerational groups and celebrations
  • Travelers who prefer an English-speaking destination
  • Waterfalls, rafting and active excursions

Mid-December through April is the driest and most comfortable stretch, and also the most popular. Summer is hot and humid with passing showers. Hurricane season runs June through November, with the highest risk generally in late summer and fall. Following Hurricane Melissa in October 2025, some properties in western and southwestern Jamaica were affected, and recovery varies by resort, so we confirm each property's current operating status before recommending it.

Getting There & Around

Making the journey simple.

By air

Sangster International in Montego Bay (MBJ) is the main gateway for resort areas. Kingston (KIN) serves the capital and eastern areas.

Transfers

Montego Bay hotels are close to the airport. Negril and Ocho Rios are typically around 1.5 to 2 hours depending on traffic and road work. We arrange private or shared transfers with vetted operators.

Getting around

Jamaicans drive on the left, and roads can be winding. Most visitors use resort transport and licensed drivers rather than renting a car.

Between resorts

If you want to combine regions, a private driver is the most comfortable way to cover distances.

Where to Stay

Choosing the right area.

Montego Bay & Falmouth

Convenient to the airport, with many resorts and easy access to Rose Hall, Martha Brae rafting and the Luminous Lagoon.

Negril

Famous for its long beach and clifftop sunsets. A relaxed, more casual atmosphere.

Ocho Rios

Lush hills, waterfalls and rainforest, with a mix of large resorts and boutique stays.

Port Antonio

Quiet, green and less developed, for travelers who want to get away from the crowds.

Good to Know

Practical details before you go.

Entry

U.S. and Canadian citizens need a valid passport. Requirements can change, and we confirm them at booking.

Safety

Most visitors stay in resort areas and travel with vetted drivers and tour operators. Before any booking we review the current U.S. and Canadian travel advisories for Jamaica, and we recommend you do too.

Money

The Jamaican dollar is the local currency. USD is widely accepted in tourist areas.

Driving

Traffic drives on the left, and roads can be narrow and winding.

Water & food

Resorts provide purified water. Drink bottled water elsewhere.

Hurricane recovery

Because of recent storm damage in parts of the island, we confirm resort and attraction status before you book.

Planning Tips

How we'd plan it.

Advisor tips

  • Choose your region first. Montego Bay, Negril and Ocho Rios each offer a distinct feel.
  • Book excursions with a vetted operator and confirm the pickup details in advance.
  • Wear water shoes for waterfalls, since rocks can be slippery.
  • Consider flexible bookings and travel insurance for summer and fall.

Questions

Common questions.

Is Jamaica safe for families?

Many families have wonderful stays by choosing resorts, vetted transport and organized tours. We review current advisories and local conditions as part of planning.

Which part of Jamaica is best for kids?

It depends on your family. Montego Bay and Falmouth are convenient and resort-rich, while Negril is relaxed and Ocho Rios is great for waterfalls.

Do we need a rental car?

Most families do not. Resort transfers and private drivers are simpler.

Is the beach good for swimming?

Many resorts have calm, swimmable beaches. We match the resort to your children's ages and swimming comfort.
