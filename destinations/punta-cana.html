Overview

Why families choose Punta Cana.

Punta Cana is the eastern tip of the Dominican Republic and one of the Caribbean's most popular all-inclusive destinations. Long, palm-fringed beaches stretch along the coast through Bávaro, Cap Cana, Uvero Alto and beyond, with large resorts that combine beach, pools, dining and entertainment under one roof.

The airport sits inside the resort region, so many families are at the beach within an hour of landing. It is a strong choice for large groups, multigenerational trips and families who want a straightforward, resort-centered vacation with a wide range of room types and budgets.

Best for

  • All-inclusive resort vacations
  • Large families and multigenerational groups
  • Families who want a short transfer from the airport
  • Groups celebrating milestones or reunions
  • Travelers who want a wide range of resort levels

The driest and most comfortable months are generally December through April. Summer is hot and humid with afternoon showers. Hurricane season runs June through November, with August through October the most active. Sargassum seaweed can reach some beaches at various times of year, particularly on the Atlantic-facing coast, and it varies by year, so we check recent conditions for the resort area we recommend.

Getting There & Around

Making the journey simple.

By air

Punta Cana International (PUJ) has nonstop flights from many U.S. and Canadian cities. Its open-air terminal is part of the experience.

Transfers

Most resorts in Punta Cana and Bávaro are 10 to 30 minutes from the airport, with Cap Cana and Uvero Alto a bit farther. Private and shared transfers are easy to arrange.

Getting around

Most families stay within the resort and take organized excursions. Taxis are available but should be agreed in advance.

Day trips

Longer excursions, such as Santo Domingo's historic colonial district, are possible but make for a long day.

Where to Stay

Choosing the right area.

Bávaro

The heart of the resort scene, with many large family resorts, shops and restaurants close to the beach.

Punta Cana & Cap Cana

More upscale, with marina, golf and polished resorts. Good for families seeking a quieter, refined stay.

Uvero Alto

A scenic stretch with dramatic coastline and large, newer resorts. Slightly longer transfer.

Miches & Samaná

Quieter, more natural areas further afield for travelers wanting a different experience.

Good to Know

Practical details before you go.

Entry

U.S. and Canadian citizens need a valid passport. The Dominican Republic has used an online entry and exit form, so we confirm the current requirements at booking.

Money

The Dominican peso is the local currency. USD is widely accepted at resorts. Carry small bills for tips.

Water & food

Drink bottled or purified water, and eat at resort restaurants or reputable local spots.

Sun

Strong sun year-round, so plan on shade breaks and reapplying sunscreen.

Safety

Stay within resort areas and use arranged transportation. Check current U.S. and Canadian travel advisories before departure.

Insurance

Because of hurricane season, trip protection is worth considering for summer and fall travel.

Planning Tips

How we'd plan it.

Advisor tips

  • Compare resorts carefully. All-inclusive does not mean identical: dining, kids' programs and beach quality vary.
  • Ask about room location. Rooms close to the main pool and restaurants save little legs a lot of walking.
  • Book excursions through a vetted operator, not from a beach vendor.
  • Consider a family or multigenerational room category for larger groups.

Questions

Common questions.

Is Punta Cana good for families with young children?

Yes. Many resorts offer kids' clubs, splash areas and family suites, and the short transfer helps with little travelers.

Is it all-inclusive everywhere?

Most resorts are all-inclusive, but inclusions differ, including premium drinks, specialty restaurants and activities. We explain the differences.

When is the best value?

Shoulder months such as late spring and early fall often have lower prices, with a higher chance of rain or storms.

How far is the beach from the airport?

Typically a short drive, which is one reason Punta Cana is so convenient.
